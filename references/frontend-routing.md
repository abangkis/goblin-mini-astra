# Frontend routing

Load before FE scouting, design, implementation, debugging, or visual and interaction testing, including small edits. Use the [shared model table](model-profiles.md) for effort selection. Only Sol and Astra execute FE work, including reproducing a product UI defect. Operating an existing app to complete a business task follows the Computer Use rule in the shared table. Luna may own a separable non-FE outcome in the same task.

## Establish what still needs design

Check the brief, approved references, existing components, and known states. Determine which decisions remain open across hierarchy, typography, spacing, density, responsive behavior, and interactions. Words such as "clean" or "premium" leave these decisions open. For a specific correction, give the owner the brief, relevant screenshots, observed gap, and a bounded acceptance check.

## Keep ownership through the first render

When visual quality is a primary outcome, the design owner carries the work through the first rendered implementation and relevant interaction checks. Hand off a mechanical remainder only when decisions are established and the transfer saves work. A Sol Coordinator may execute a small fix directly at its current effort. A Coordinator outside Sol/Astra owns scope and final acceptance using evidence from an eligible executor. If none is available, report the blocked FE outcome.

## Verify the experience

Inspect rendered desktop and mobile screens and exercise the primary interaction. Evaluate hierarchy, readability, consistency, responsive behavior, and the states required by the brief. Reuse valid evidence and check new interactions after integration. A passing build alone leaves visual acceptance unverified.
